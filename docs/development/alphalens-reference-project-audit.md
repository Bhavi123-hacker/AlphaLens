# AlphaLens reference-project audit

Reviewed public primary documentation on 17 October 2026. This is a design audit, not independent verification of another project's advertised performance. No external source code, model, dataset, paid download or account was acquired. No dependency or upstream market-data permission follows from a README or code licence.

Categories: **A** means a useful engineering/research principle already applicable to AlphaLens; **B** means an idea requiring its own registered experiment and measured evidence; **C** means a service, claim or shortcut not adopted.

## Optional fundamentals and news

**Current descriptive reference is separate from historical features.** The existing private `workers/providers/reference.py` explicitly loads current company/profile, ratio and action observations into expiring memory. It declares historical PIT eligibility false, raw retention false and action completeness false. This audit made no provider requests and did not expand that functionality.

[Upstox's Company Profile documentation](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-company-profile/) describes company reference data. Its existence does not establish a historical point-in-time financial statement archive with release, availability and revision clocks. [Official Corporate Actions documentation](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-corporate-actions/) supplies event names and dates; an empty response does not prove an action-free historical interval. This is a documented research limitation, not a substitute adjustment factor.

Fundamental PIT features, historical sector-relative features and news-sentiment model features remain **UNAVAILABLE / NOT ENABLED** unless source-specific use rights, dated observations, availability clocks, coverage and reproducibility are demonstrated. No absent feature becomes a neutral value. Existing OHLCV-based models are unchanged by this audit.

[NewsAPI's official pricing and usage rules](https://newsapi.org/pricing) limit the free Developer service to development/testing and expressly exclude staging or production, including internal production. Its free results are delayed and historically limited; this is not a suitable free operational news dependency for AlphaLens. [NewsAPI terms](https://newsapi.org/terms) also do not establish that the publishers' full articles may be freely retained or used for model training. No NewsAPI account, request, scraping job or paid upgrade was created.

The appropriate result today is a working price-based research system with explicit missing fundamental/news evidence. Optional future inputs require a separately qualified free source; they do not justify weakening the existing private-source, chronology, acceptance or public-demo boundaries.
