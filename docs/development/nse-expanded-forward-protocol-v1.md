# Expanded current-cohort prospective research protocol v1

Registered before this protocol's first live declaration and before its future
entry opens. Registration is the exact SHA-256 pinned by the implementation.
Historical V1/V2 evaluations remain known development information. Nothing here
reclassifies their failure, promotes a model, or changes original acceptance.

The study uses the separately retained, authorized current NSE common-equity
cohort. It is fixed-current-cohort research, not survivorship-free history or an
investment adviser service. The original V2 price-only model, ordered sixteen
features and source-compatible five-session price-component target are unchanged.
Out-of-training-cohort extrapolation remains explicitly unvalidated.

Each declaration freezes actual declaration time, the exact scored snapshot,
model and dataset IDs, source and feature hashes, each actual score and the frozen
majority, momentum, mean-reversion and risk baselines. No supplied historical
decision timestamp is accepted. Every declared next open must be after fresh
independent clock upper bounds. A declaration links the already immutable
per-security forecast IDs; it never rewrites those forecasts. Different snapshots
remain distinct declarations and must not be counted as independent decision dates.

For the declared cohort, rank each score/baseline descending with exact instrument
key as the deterministic tie breaker. Freeze Top 5, 10 and 20, capped by genuinely
scored coverage. Compare equal-weight price-component returns against the entire
declared eligible cohort, not an index, current constituents, or a shard average.
Do not recompute selection after outcomes appear. User preference filtering is a
separate descriptive view and does not change this frozen evaluation cohort.

The target is next eligible session open to the fifth subsequent eligible session
close. Prices are source-qualified split/bonus-adjusted price components, excluding
dividends. Require the existing per-security official-session, source-retrieval,
identity, corporate-action, maturity and immutable-revision checks. Missing or
excluded outcomes remain visible. Global metrics and selection comparisons stay
pending until every declared member has an eligible matured outcome; missing Top-N
members must never be dropped to improve results. Revisions append new result
versions and preserve prior results.

Report balanced accuracy, Brier score and rank information correlation for the
complete cohort and each frozen directional baseline. Report equal-weight gross
price-component return and assumed net sensitivity for the frozen Top-N sets.
The predeclared illustrative cost is 25 basis points round-trip plus 5 basis points
slippage on each side (35 basis points total per new position). It is an assumption,
not a verified fill, fee schedule, realized profit or total shareholder return.
Turnover, drawdown across overlapping declarations and independent-date uncertainty
remain unavailable until a separate correctly timed multi-date portfolio path is
defined; do not infer them from single-declaration endpoint returns. Shared-date
stock outcomes are not independent samples. No significance or edge claim follows
from a single completed date or selection from many known experiments.

The original independent acceptance requirements remain unchanged: at least 126
matured decision dates, 1,000 eligible outcomes, 30 eligible securities, two quarters,
all registered statistical/economic/robustness requirements, and documented actual
independent review. This declaration/result service never writes the accepted
rankings or approval state. Accepted predictions remain unavailable until those
separate publication gates genuinely pass.
