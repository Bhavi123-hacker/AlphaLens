## Daily operation and source scope

The workflow is `workers.experimental_nse_v2.workflow`, invoked by
`scripts/windows/run-v2-research.cmd`. It does not train, trade, publish accepted
rankings or make provider calls from a dashboard refresh. It uses the existing
per-run independent date/clock guard and official dated market timings; an
intraday quote classification does not gate retained EOD collection. Raw clocks
and receipts are unchanged. Unauthenticated NTP assumptions remain visible.

Official Upstox private historical-retention/derived-ML scope is recorded in
[the source audit](../data/ml-v2-source-and-target-audit.md), including the
[specific staff permission](https://community.upstox.com/t/can-upstox-historical-api-data-be-retained-locally-for-private-ml-backtesting-research/17302/3).
This is private noncommercial research, not public redistribution/adviser rights.
Transient action inspection retains minimal derived exclusion facts, not raw
Fundamentals bodies; completeness remains unknown. OHLC has provider-staff
split/bonus-adjustment evidence, excludes dividends, and is not double-adjusted.

The exact disabled proposal, bound to the real protected configuration, is:

- Separate `AlphaLens-Private-V2-Research` at 20:30, 21:30 and 22:30 IST.
- At most 120 GET attempts/run and 360/day **for V2**, three collection
  reservations/IST day, at least 60 independently bounded minutes apart.
- One oldest missing official session/run, maximum 31 calendar days catch-up;
  at most ten dated calendar lookups/run. Zero automatic transport retries;
  later publication attempts obey the same quota/cooldown. Existing provider
  account rate limits and the separate RELIANCE budget still apply.

  StartWhenAvailable. Missed triggers may therefore run at the next login,
  outside the evening slots, within the same caps. Windows and the same
  credential-owning operator must be available. No publication-time guarantee.
